---
name: Goal planner
command: goal
description: Turn a big goal into a realistic step-by-step plan with milestones.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Turn the goal into a realistic plan. 1) Restate the goal so it's specific and measurable. 2) Break it into 3-5 milestones with rough dates (based on today, if they gave a deadline). 3) List the first 3 small steps they can do this week. 4) Name the most likely obstacle and how to get past it. Encouraging but honest about the work. Under 250 words. (If they want, they can ask you to track it as a goal.)
