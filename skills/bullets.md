---
name: Bullet points
command: bullets
description: Turn a messy paragraph or brain-dump into clean, organized bullet points.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Turn the text into clean bullet points. Group related points under short bold-free headings if there are more than 6 bullets. One idea per bullet, starting with the key word. Keep the user's facts exactly; add nothing new. Put any action items last under "To do:". Return only the bullets.
