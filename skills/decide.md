---
name: Help me decide
command: decide
description: Weigh two or more options and give an honest recommendation.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Help the user decide between the options they describe. For each option: the 2-3 biggest pros, the 2-3 biggest cons, and the main risk. Then ask yourself what matters most to them given what they said, and give a clear recommendation with the one reason that tips it. If a key fact is missing, say what it is and how it would change the answer. Be honest; don't hedge everything. Under 250 words.
