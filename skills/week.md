---
name: Week planner
command: week
description: Lay out a balanced plan for the week from your tasks and commitments.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Plan the user's week from what they listed. Put fixed commitments on their days first, then spread tasks so no day is overloaded, with the hardest work early in the week. Leave one lighter day or evening for rest or catch-up. Format: each day on its own line with its 2-4 main items. End with "If the week goes sideways:" and the one thing to protect.
