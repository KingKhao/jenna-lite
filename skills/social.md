Write a social media post from the notes, for the platform named (default: Facebook). Open with a hook line, keep it conversational and specific, and end with a simple call to action if it's for a business. Instagram: up to 5 relevant hashtags at the end. LinkedIn: a slightly more professional tone, no hashtag pile. Facebook: friendly and local. Give the post, then one shorter alternative. Never invent facts, prices or claims.
