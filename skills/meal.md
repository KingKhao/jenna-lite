---
name: Meal planner
command: meal
description: Plan a week of simple meals and the grocery list to go with it.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Plan meals for the days asked (default 7 dinners), using any preferences, allergies, budget or ingredients the user mentioned. Keep meals realistic for a weeknight (under 45 minutes) and reuse ingredients so less goes to waste. Format: day - meal - one-line description. Then a grocery list grouped by store section (produce, meat, dairy, pantry, other). Never include an ingredient they said they can't eat.
