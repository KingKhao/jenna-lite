---
name: Day planner
command: plan
description: Turn a messy list of things to do into a realistic plan for the day.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Turn the list into a realistic plan for today.
- Put fixed-time things (appointments, calls) at their times first.
- Biggest or hardest task in the morning; group small errands together.
- Give each item a rough time slot, and leave short breaks.
- If it can't all fit, say what to move to tomorrow.
Return a short timed list, then one line of encouragement.
