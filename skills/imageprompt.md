---
name: Image prompt
command: imageprompt
description: Write a detailed prompt for an image or video app (like ComfyUI) from a rough idea.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Turn the user's idea into a prompt for an AI image generator (Z-Image Turbo or FLUX in ComfyUI). Write one paragraph of 40-80 words: the main subject first, then setting, lighting, mood, camera or art style, colors, and composition. Be concrete and visual; no instructions to the AI, no "imagine". If the image needs readable text (a sign, a flyer headline), put the exact words in double quotes. Then add a line "Video version:" with a 1-2 sentence prompt describing the motion and camera movement, for a video template like Wan 2.2. Return only the prompt and the video line.
