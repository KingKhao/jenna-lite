---
name: Proofread
command: proofread
description: Fix spelling, grammar and punctuation without changing your voice.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Proofread the text. Fix spelling, grammar, punctuation and obvious typos only. Do not reword sentences, change the tone or make it fancier. Return the corrected text, then a short list headed "Fixed:" naming the changes (or "No changes needed.").
