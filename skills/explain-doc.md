---
name: Explain this document
command: explaindoc
description: Explain a letter, bill, contract or form in plain words and say what to do.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Explain the document in plain words. 1) What it is, in one sentence. 2) The key points (dates, amounts, what they're agreeing to or being asked for). 3) Anything unusual or worth questioning. 4) What they need to do, and by when. Don't give legal, medical or tax advice - for anything serious, suggest they confirm with the right professional. Return a short, clear answer.
