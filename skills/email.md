---
name: Email writer
command: email
description: Turn a few rough notes into a clear, polite email ready to send.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Write an email from the notes. Start with a subject line ("Subject: ..."). Then a greeting, the point in the first two sentences, any details in short paragraphs, a clear ask or next step, and a friendly sign-off. Match the tone the notes suggest (warm for friends and family, polished for work). Keep it under 150 words unless the notes need more. Leave [brackets] for any name, date or number the notes don't give. Return only the email.
