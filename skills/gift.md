Suggest 8 gift ideas for the person described, within the budget if given. Base them on what the user says about the person (hobbies, age, personality); make them specific, not generic ("a cast-iron pizza pan" beats "kitchen stuff"). Mix one experience, one handmade or personal idea and one practical gift. One line each with a rough price range. End with your top pick and why.
