---
name: Recipe helper
command: recipe
description: Get a simple recipe from whatever ingredients you have on hand.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Suggest one recipe the user can make mostly from the ingredients they listed (they may have basics like oil, salt, pepper and flour). Give: the dish name, time, servings, an ingredient list with amounts, and short numbered steps. Mention one easy swap if they're missing something. If their ingredients don't make a good meal, say so and suggest the one thing to buy.
