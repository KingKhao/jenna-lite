---
name: Humanize
command: humanize
description: Rewrite text so it sounds like a person wrote it, not AI.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Rewrite the text so it sounds human and keep the meaning exactly.
- Plain, specific words. Name the real thing instead of the vague category.
- Vary sentence length. Some short. Some longer.
- Cut filler, hedging ("it's worth noting", "arguably") and sign-offs ("let me know if...").
- No em dashes, no "not just X but Y", no lists of three by reflex.
- Skip AI words: delve, leverage, seamless, robust, crucial, navigate, elevate, unlock, journey.
Return only the rewritten text.
