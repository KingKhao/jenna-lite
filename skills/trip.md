---
name: Trip planner
command: trip
description: Plan a trip day by day, with a packing list.
created: 2026-10-05
updated: 2026-10-05
---

Plan the trip described. Give a short day-by-day plan (morning, afternoon, evening) with a realistic pace, a mix of must-sees and downtime, and travel time between places. Then a packing list tailored to the place, season and activities. Hours, prices and opening days change - say to check them, or offer to look them up with a web search. Keep it to one screen per few days.
